## 2026.11.10

- **Redstone pipes have channels (all ten builds).** Every face of a redstone pipe is tuned to a channel, shown as a row of dye-colored buttons on the pipe screen. A signal only travels between faces on the same channel, so two redstone lines can share the same pipes without mixing. A pipe offers 1, 2, 4, 8, 12 or 16 channels by tier (copper to fluxium); faces start on channel 0, so existing pipes behave as before.
- **Flux Stress converter on 1.20.1 (Forge and NeoForge).** With Create 6.0 installed, the six-tier Stress converter turns a shaft from Flux, as it already does on 1.21.1 NeoForge. It has a screen, a turning shaft, recipes and loot tables, and shows in Jade, The One Probe and WTHIT. Without Create it does not exist.
- Other builds: no change in play beyond the channels.
