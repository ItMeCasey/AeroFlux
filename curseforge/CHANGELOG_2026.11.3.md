## 2026.11.3

- **Fix:** the Infuser now takes Aero Flux as well as Moon Flux. In 2026.11.2 the new Sawdust to Flux Dust recipe, which runs on Aero Flux, could not run because the Infuser's tank refused Aero Flux. The crystal and crying obsidian recipes still need Moon Flux.

Everything in 2026.11.2 is included: machines named `<Tier> <Machine>`, tanks that take any fluid, the Plant Grower soil slot, the early-game Infuser recipes, the new guidebook and tool art, and optional Show Me What This Is support on Forge 26.1.2.
