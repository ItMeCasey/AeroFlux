# Next machine wave: concepts and art

Ten new blocks, all with six tiers (copper, iron, gold, diamond, netherite, fluxium), all Mekanism-style
casings with 32 px textures. Art is generated by `assets-src/tools/gen_new_machines.py` and lives next to this
file. Everything here is art plus a design spec; the Java, recipes and wiring belong to the build session.

Shared rules for every machine below: the standard `FluxMachineBlockEntity` base (slots, one Flux tank,
progress, upgrade slots, side config, redstone mode), `facing` + `lit` blockstates, animated `_front_active`
strip while working. "Tier" scales speed and buffer the same way the existing machines do.

## 1. Flux Crafter (`flux_crafter`, Aero)
Auto crafting table. A 3x3 ghost-pattern grid in the screen; take the recipe from a pattern slot or from
the vanilla recipe you set, pull ingredients from the input slots/pipes, push the result out. Tier: crafts
per second. Window shows the grid with a scan highlight while working and the result slot flashing on finish.

## 2. Flux Purifier (`flux_purifier`, Aero + water)
The ore multiplier. Ore (or raw ore) + a little water + Flux turns into 2x clean output (3x with a Speed-style
Purity upgrade), then a furnace or the Alloy Smelter finishes it. Recipe type `aeroflux:purifying`, KubeJS
and JEI like the other machines. Window: spray nozzles over an ore basket with a water pool.

## 3. Moon Spawner (`moon_spawner`, Moon)
Controlled mob farm. Put a spawn egg in the slot; it spawns that mob inside a kill chamber and drops loot into
the output. Upgrades: Looting, Speed. Config: max mobs per cycle, a switch to disable it on servers, no
player-kill credit unless the Looting upgrade is installed. Tier: cycle time. Window: barred chamber with eyes
that blink while it runs.

## 4. Sky Controller (`sky_controller`, Aero + Moon)
Sets time and weather. Three buttons in the GUI: Day (costs Aero Flux), Night (costs Moon Flux), Clear/Rain.
Per-use cost plus a cooldown, both in config, and a config switch to turn it off for servers. Tier: shorter
cooldown. Window: a sun/moon split sky that cycles through cloud, rain and moonlight while it works.

## 5. Flux Blender (`flux_blender`, Aero + Moon)
Two tank inputs, one Twilight Flux output. A new fluid `aeroflux:twilight_flux`, violet-pink, used by top-tier
recipes (fluxium upgrades, the Sky Controller at higher tiers, anything you want gated). Ratio 1:1 in, 1 out at
a small Flux cost. Window: gold tank, blue tank, mixing bowl with a paddle.

## 6. Animal Breeder (`animal_breeder`, Aero)
Feeds animals in range and breeds them, then drops the babies (or leaves them, a config choice). Put the food in
the slot. Range scales with tier (3 up to 9 blocks), cap on animals per area to stop lag. Window: hay pen with
a trough, a pig and a cow, and a heart that pulses.

## 7. Flux Repairer (`flux_repairer`, Moon)
Repairs tools and armor from XP. Takes a damaged item plus an XP source (a bottle of enchanting, or XP fluid when
that mod is present), repairs it, and keeps enchantments. Does not stack with Mending beyond normal limits.
Window: anvil, hammer strike with sparks, a green XP orb.

## 8. Flux Sifter (`flux_sifter`, Aero), the tiered sieve
The tier IS the mesh. The tray shows the real mesh color from above, so you can tell a tier at a glance.

| Tier | Mesh level | Mesh color |
|---|---|---|
| copper | string | tan |
| iron | flint | dark gray |
| gold | iron | silver |
| diamond | diamond | cyan |
| netherite | netherite | black-brown |
| fluxium | netherite + bonus | glowing Aero gold |

Rule: a sifter runs every recipe whose required mesh level is at or below its own. Rank meshes in a config list
(string < flint < iron < emerald < diamond < netherite) and rank any unknown modded mesh by its position in that
list. Fluxium runs all of them, with a configurable bonus (default +25% chance and 2x speed).

Compat (soft dependency, no hard requirement on either mod):
- **Ex Deorum:** recipe type `exdeorum:sieve`; fields `ingredient`, `mesh`, `result`, `result_amount` and the optional
  `by_hand_only` (skip those: the machine must not run them).
- **Ex Nihilo Sequentia:** recipe type `exnihilosequentia:sieve`; fields are the input block or tag, the result, a
  `rolls` list where each roll has a `chance` and the `mesh` it needs, and an optional `waterlogged` flag.
  `waterlogged` recipes need water in the sifter's fluid tank (50 mB per run).
- Field names differ by version (the pages I checked cover 1.20.1 and Ex Nihilo 4.2.0), so confirm against the
  installed jars before coding the parsers.
- Without either mod the machine still works: ship a small built-in `aeroflux:sifting` recipe set (gravel,
  sand, soul sand, dust) so it is never a dead block.

Window: a mesh with a heap of material shaking on it and grains dropping through into a pile.

## 9. Flux Magnet (`flux_magnet`, Moon)
Item vacuum. Pulls dropped items in a radius (tier sets 4 to 16 blocks) into its inventory and out through the
pipe faces. A filter slot and a whitelist toggle keep it from eating junk. Model: a real horseshoe magnet
lying in an open bowl, red and blue poles toward the front.

## 10. Sun Lamp and Moon Lamp (`sun_lamp`, `moon_lamp`, no machine UI)
Caged crystal lamps, not full cubes, `lit` state only. Powered by a little Flux from a pipe or an adjacent tank.
- Sun Lamp: no hostile spawns in the radius and crops inside grow faster. Radius scales with tier (4 to 16).
- Moon Lamp: the same spawn prevention, and it keeps night-only plants growing in daylight.
Light level 15 when lit, 0 when out of Flux.

## Build order I'd suggest
Crafter, Purifier, Lamps and Sifter first (most used), then Blender (unlocks Twilight Flux), Sky Controller,
Magnet, Breeder, Repairer, Spawner.
