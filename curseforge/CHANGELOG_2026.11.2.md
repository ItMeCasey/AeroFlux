## 2026.11.2

Machines and tanks you can name without the Flux:

- **Machines are renamed** `<Tier> <Machine>`: Copper Furnace, Netherite Sawmill, Magnet and so on. Only the names changed, so existing worlds are not affected.
- **Tanks take any fluid**, one at a time, not only Aero and Moon Flux. The screen draws the fluid with its own texture and name, a broken tank keeps its fluid on the item, and The One Probe and WTHIT show it.
- **Plant Grower soil slot:** a second slot takes the soil. Crops want farmland, saplings and the rest take dirt, grass or farmland; data packs can add soils with growth or seed bonuses, and Mystical Agriculture essence farmland gives extra seeds.
- **Early game:** the Infuser is built with a copper ingot, and it turns Sawdust into Flux Dust with Aero Flux.
- **Guidebook:** 23 pages in six chapters with a contents page, and buttons that open the wiki.
- **Tools:** a simpler Conduit Tool and Flux Configurator that sit upright in the hand.
- **Show Me What This Is:** on Forge 26.1.2, AeroFlux's lines show in this look-at overlay when it is installed.

This is also the first public build for NeoForge and Forge 1.21.1, NeoForge and Forge 1.20.1, and Forge 26.1.2.
