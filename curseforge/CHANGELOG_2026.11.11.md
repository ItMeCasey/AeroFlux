## 2026.11.11

- **Fixed mouse clicks on Minecraft 26.3 (Forge and NeoForge).** Minecraft 26.3 renumbered the mouse buttons (left is now 1, middle 2, right 3), and the mod still compared clicks against 0 and 1. Ghost slots, the redstone mode button and the side-configuration panel (left click to open, right click to cycle backwards) now respond to the right buttons. Only the 26.3 builds are in this release; the other builds are unchanged.
